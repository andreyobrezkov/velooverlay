Customize Widgets Without Rebuilding Your Layout

Good telemetry overlays should match your footage, not fight it. VeloOverlay Toolkit lets you start with a polished preset and then fine-tune each widget only where it matters.

Start with a preset

Presets give you a consistent visual baseline across the whole HUD. They are useful when you want to:

  • switch a project from subtle to bold in seconds
  • keep shared colors and accents consistent across widgets
  • preserve a few hand-tuned overrides while the rest of the HUD follows the preset

Then customize individual widgets

Open the Widgets sidebar to add and edit overlay elements such as speed, maps, altitude, heart rate, and more.

Once a widget is selected, you can adjust details like:

  • colors and accent colors
  • opacity and borders
  • fonts and sizing
  • layout and placement on the canvas

A practical way to work

  1. Pick a preset that fits the overall feel of the clip.
  2. Add your core widgets.
  3. Tweak only the widgets that need extra contrast or emphasis.
  4. Check the live preview against bright and dark parts of the footage.

Because the preview updates immediately, you can dial in readability before running a final render.
