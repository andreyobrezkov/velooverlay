Desktop App for Offline Telemetry Editing

If you prefer working with local files instead of browser uploads, the desktop app is the most direct way to use VeloOverlay Toolkit.

Why use the desktop app

The desktop version is designed for creators working with large action camera files and longer renders. It keeps the entire workflow on your machine so you can move quickly without waiting for uploads or re-downloads.

That means:

  • local access to your video and telemetry files
  • the same editor workflow as the web experience
  • a better fit for offline work and heavier projects

Best use cases

The desktop app is especially handy when you are:

  • editing long rides recorded in multiple camera files
  • working with high-bitrate GoPro footage
  • exporting often and wanting a predictable local workflow
  • traveling or editing without reliable internet

What to expect

You still get the same core process: import footage, sync telemetry, place widgets, preview the result, and render locally. The difference is that the whole setup feels more natural when your assets already live on disk.

If your main goal is privacy, speed, and fewer moving parts, start here.
