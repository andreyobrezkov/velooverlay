Getting Started

April 7, 2026

Welcome to VeloOverlay Toolkit

VeloOverlay Toolkit is built for riders, runners, and action camera creators who want clean telemetry overlays without shipping giant video files to the cloud.

If you already have a camera clip and a .fit or .gpx recording, you can bring them together in one offline workflow: import, align, design, preview, and render.

Why people switch to an offline workflow

Cloud tools are fine for quick experiments, but they get painful when your ride footage is large or your internet connection is not.

With VeloOverlay Toolkit, everything stays on your machine:

  • No video uploads before you can start editing
  • No subscription requirement just to export a ride clip
  • Full privacy for your footage and location data
  • Fast local rendering using your own hardware

What you can do today

  • Import action camera footage and telemetry files
  • Visually line up your data with your video on a timeline
  • Add speed, map, elevation, heart rate, and other widgets
  • Preview your layout before committing to a full render
  • Export full videos, clips, and still images for sharing

A good place to start

If this is your first project, begin with these workflows:

  1. Load your main video and telemetry file.
  2. Open Trim & Align to sync the footage and data.
  3. Add widgets from the Widgets sidebar.
  4. Use the preview to fine-tune placement and styling.
  5. Export a short clip or still image to test your setup.

The rest of this blog walks through each part of that process in more detail.
