Overlay Studio & Race History: Photos, Reels, and a Home for Your Results

VeloOverlay started as a way to map GPS data onto your video. It still does that better than anything else—but it is not the whole story anymore. Two features turn it into a complete sharing toolkit: Overlay Studio for photos and Reels, and Race History for keeping your results in one place.

Overlay Studio: stats on a photo

You do not always have video. Sometimes you have one great photo from the finish line, a summit, or a fast descent—and you want it to carry the same telemetry context your videos do.

Overlay Studio composites your activity stats and a traced GPS route directly onto a photo, then exports a high-resolution PNG. You get:

  • 17 themes — from Strava and Garmin looks to Cyberpunk, Neon, Mono, and Ironman.
  • 5 structural layouts — Stat Band, Hero Number, The Stack, Race Result, plus the adaptive classic placement.
  • 11 display fonts and 5 aspect ratios (9:16, 4:5, 1:1, 16:9, and the original photo size).

Pick a theme, drop in your numbers, position the route trace, and export. It runs in your browser or as an Android app, so you can do it straight from your phone.

Reels without a video render

If a single frame is not enough, switch to Reel mode. It builds a vertical Instagram Reel entirely on your device—no full video render, no cloud upload.

Each Reel is a 20–30 second, 1080×1920 MP4 at 30fps with:

  • your GPS track animating over a live map (or a stylized background when you are offline),
  • highlight callouts for your peak speed, heart rate, power, and biggest climbs,
  • and your photos popping in at the exact spot on the route where they were taken.

The export happens locally through WebCodecs, so it works the same in the browser and in the offline app.

Race History keeps your results

Your results deserve better than a screenshot folder. Race History is a searchable library of every race you have finished. Get races in three ways:

  • Import from sportos.ee — paste your athlete profile URL and pull your full result list automatically.
  • Add manually — type in a race by hand whenever you need to.
  • Link to Strava — auto-match a race to a Strava activity by the same calendar day and distance (±10%), or link any activity by its ID.

Each entry stores the event, date, sport, finish time, distance, pace, your overall / category / gender placements, and your bib, rider name, and team. You can attach a finish photo to any race and open it full-size, then filter the whole library by sport and year.

How they work together

This is where it clicks. When you build a photo or a Reel in Overlay Studio, the Race Results block can pull straight from your library: pick a stored race and its finish time and placements drop in automatically—no retyping numbers off a results page.

So the workflow becomes: import your races once, then reuse them on every photo and Reel you make. One library, feeding everything.

Try it

Overlay Studio is free, works offline, and needs no subscription. Open it in your browser or install the Android app from the download page, import a few races, and turn your next ride into something worth sharing.
