Auto-Sync GoPro Video With Your Telemetry

If your video and telemetry started at different moments, you do not always need to sync them by hand.

What Auto Sync does

Auto Sync extracts GPS data from a supported GoPro clip and compares it with your FIT track. The editor then suggests the telemetry start offset that best matches the route. That gives you a faster starting point when manual alignment would normally take several rounds of scrubbing.

When it helps most

Auto Sync is especially useful when you:

  • started your bike computer before pressing record
  • forgot the exact moment the activity began
  • want a quick first pass before doing final visual checks

The workflow

  1. Load a GoPro video as your main clip.
  2. Load your telemetry file.
  3. Open the Auto Sync section in the editor.
  4. Extract GPS from the video.
  5. Run Auto Sync and review the suggested offset.
  6. Apply the result, then confirm it in the preview.

Good to know

The editor also reports a confidence level, which helps you decide whether the match is trustworthy or whether the route data needs a manual review. It is a great time-saver, but the best practice is still the same: apply the suggestion, then watch a clear movement moment in the preview to confirm everything lines up.
